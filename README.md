### 🔗 [FwdLink](https://fwdlink.io)

**AI Visibility Intelligence · B2B SaaS**

FwdLink helps businesses understand how AI-powered search engines discover, evaluate, and recommend their brands.

As AI assistants increasingly influence purchasing decisions, FwdLink provides insights into how brands appear in AI-generated answers.

**Key Capabilities**
- Analyze brand visibility across AI assistants
- Benchmark brands against competitors
- Identify sources influencing AI recommendations
- Discover opportunities to improve AI search visibility
- Monitor changes in brand visibility over time

**My Role:** Product strategy, system architecture, full-stack development, AI integration, and cloud deployment.

**Website:** https://fwdlink.io

### 🐜 [BullAnt](https://bullant.io)

**Multiplayer Browser Game · Virtual Economy · RPG**

BullAnt is a real-time browser game combining simulated financial markets, real estate investment, and RPG progression.

Players begin as small ants and gradually build their wealth through financial decisions, investments, and multiplayer interactions.

**Key Features**
- Simulated stock trading and portfolio management
- Virtual real estate investment and rental income
- Loans, leverage, and financial risk management
- RPG progression, equipment, and dungeon battles
- Player-driven economy and multiplayer interactions

**My Role:** Game design, full-stack development, backend architecture, and service operations.

**Website:** https://bullant.io
